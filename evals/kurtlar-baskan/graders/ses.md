This is a "Kurtlar Konseyi" debate in Turkish where members speak as Kurtlar Vadisi characters.
PASS if ALL of these hold:
- The characters sound recognisably like themselves: İplikçi Nedim uses a merchant tone with at most one signature
  expression such as "kuzum", "canim" or "vallahi, Allah seni inandırsın"; Laz Ziya uses Black Sea dialect touches
  ("uşağum", "ha bu", "da") and/or a stage note for his famous laugh such as "(o meşhur gülüşüyle)" — a laugh spelled out in letters ("hehe") is NOT expected; Hüsrev Ağa speaks as a wise elder ("evlat", proverbs).
- It is NOT cringe: no member repeats signature phrases more than once, dialect is a light touch rather than
  every word, and every member still makes a concrete, substantive argument about the coffee-shop decision.
- No threats, violence or crime are suggested.
FAIL if the dialogue is a caricature (catchphrase spam, heavy dialect in every sentence), if a member has style but
no real argument, or if the characters sound generic.
